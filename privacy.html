Privacy

Last updated October 24, 2026

The short version: this website doesn't use cookies, doesn't track you, has no ads and no analytics, and doesn't ask for any personal information.

What this website collects

Nothing on purpose. There are no forms, no accounts, no cookies and no tracking or analytics tools on this site.

Services this website uses

Like every website, this one has to be delivered by someone. These services receive basic technical information, such as your IP address and browser type, when you visit:

  • GitHub Pages hosts the site. GitHub may keep short-term logs for security. See GitHub's privacy statement.
  • Cloudflare runs the address octopusheartsoftware.com and forwards email sent to our support address. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts supplies the lettering on this site, so your browser asks Google for the font files. See Google Fonts' privacy notes.

If you email us

When you write to support@octopusheartsoftware.com, we receive your email address and whatever you include in the message. Mail to that address is forwarded through Cloudflare to our Gmail inbox, so Google stores it too. We use your message only to reply and to improve our apps. We never sell it, share it for marketing or add you to a mailing list. If you'd like us to delete your messages, just ask.

Our apps

Each app has its own privacy policy, which explains exactly what that app uses and why. We'll post each one here before the app is released.

Children

This website isn't aimed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes and questions

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top. Questions? Email support@octopusheartsoftware.com.
