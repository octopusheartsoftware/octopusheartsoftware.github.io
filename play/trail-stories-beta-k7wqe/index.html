Trail Stories test

A desk test of one section of the Appalachian Trail. It doesn't use your real location; you move along the trail with the slider. Nothing you do here leaves your device.

Where the information comes from

  • Trail line and shelters: National Park Service and Appalachian Trail Conservancy
  • Elevation: USGS 3D Elevation Program
  • Plants and animals: GBIF.org, only records free for any use (CC0 and CC BY), with each photo's author shown
  • Rocks: Macrostrat (CC BY 4.0)
  • Treaty records: US Forest Service, from Royce's Indian Land Cessions (1899)
  • Old photos and drawings: Library of Congress
  • Nearby places: Wikipedia (CC BY-SA)
  • Stories: written for this test from the sources listed under each one. They're drafts and still need a fact check.
