How it works
  1. Tap a bar in the strip. Tap another to stretch the selection.
  2. Press Play. Those bars loop over and over.
  3. Play along. What you play comes back each time round, and you can add more.
  4. Press Keep when you like it. Then pick other bars or another instrument.

On a computer: Space plays or stops, Enter keeps. Drums: Q W E R / A S D F. Keys: A W S E D F T G Y H U J, with Z and X for octaves.
