How to play
  • Quick jab (a wooden “tock” and a glint on his glove): tap Block as it lands.
  • Big swing (a rising whoosh and a red brush circle growing behind him): tap Dodge as it lands. Blocking it breaks your guard.
  • Dodging a big swing, or a perfect block, leaves him open. Tap Attack on the beat: each beat in a row hits harder and keeps him open, and the 4th beat in a row is a Full Measure finisher.
  • Punching while he’s guarding gets you countered.
  • Landing hits fills your Rhythm bar. When it’s full, tap the strip along the top for Flow: three quick combos. Punch the arrows on the half-beats as the red line reaches them (← Block zone, → Attack zone, ↓ Dodge strip). Land every arrow for a finishing blow.
  • On a computer: ← Block, → Attack, ↓ or Space to Dodge, ↑ for Flow.

Timing check

Phones add a tiny delay to sound and touch. Tap the circle along with the clicks: 4 to get ready, then tap along with the next 12.
