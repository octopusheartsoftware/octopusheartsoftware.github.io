Hello from Octopus Heart Software

We're a one-person studio in Missouri making apps and games. They're friendly, honest about what they can and can't do, and careful with your data.

What we care about

Every app we make follows the same four promises.

  • Honest

    We say plainly what an app does and what it can't do yet. No tricks, no fake urgency.

  • Safe

    We collect only what an app needs and keep it on your device where we can. No pop-up or full-screen ads.

  • Friendly

    Clear, everyday words, and help when something goes wrong.

  • Community

    Your feedback shapes what we build next, and we credit the people and data sources behind our apps.
