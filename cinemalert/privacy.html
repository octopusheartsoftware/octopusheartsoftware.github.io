CinemAlert Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026 · CinemAlert by Octopus Heart Software LLC

The short version: CinemAlert has no accounts, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't show ads or ask where you are. Your alerts, reminders and settings are saved only on your device. Octopus Heart Software does not collect, receive, sell or share any personal information about you.

What's saved on your device

To work, CinemAlert saves these things in your device's own app storage. They are never sent to us:

  • your alerts, reminders, and the movies you hid with "Not interested"
  • your weekly summary settings, and which movies you've already seen in the app
  • a copy of the latest movie list

Deleting the app (or clearing its data) erases all of it. If your phone backs up its apps to your Google account, CinemAlert's saved data can be part of that backup like any app's; that backup is between you and Google, and we never see it.

Services the app contacts

CinemAlert talks to a few outside services to get movie information. Like any website, these services can see your device's internet (IP) address when the app contacts them. CinemAlert sends them only what's listed here and never sends your alerts or reminders anywhere.

  • Octopus Heart Software website (octopusheartsoftware.com, hosted by GitHub Pages): a request for the shared movie list, to download what's in and coming to US theaters. The list is the same for everyone and is built from TMDB data.
  • TMDB image servers (image.tmdb.org): requests for movie posters.
  • Octopus Heart feedback service (feedback.octopusheartsoftware.com, run on Cloudflare): only what you type into the Send feedback form, and only when you press Send (see below).

Links that open other sites

Some buttons open another website or app, whose own privacy policy then applies: Find showtimes (Google Search), Trailer (YouTube), Add opening day to calendar (Google Calendar), and the score links to IMDb, Rotten Tomatoes and Letterboxd.

Notifications

If you turn on the weekly summary, your device checks the shared movie list once a week and compares it with your alerts on your device. No server learns your alerts, and no push-notification service is used. If you tap "Remind me" on a film that hasn't opened yet, the app also sets a reminder on your device for its opening day; that too stays on the device.

Sending feedback

"Send feedback" opens a short form inside the app. Nothing is sent unless you press Send. If you do, we receive your message, the type you picked (bug, idea and so on), the app version, your type of device, when your movie list was last updated, and your email address only if you chose to type it in. The form sends this through our feedback service, a small program we run on Cloudflare at feedback.octopusheartsoftware.com, which emails it to our support inbox and keeps no copy of its own; Cloudflare's own privacy policy covers that hop. We use what you send only to reply and to improve CinemAlert. We don't share it, and we delete it on request. If you'd rather email us directly, the form shows the support address and can open your own email app instead.

Children

CinemAlert is a general-audience app. It does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Data sources and credits

Movie data and posters come from TMDB. This product uses TMDB and the TMDB APIs but is not endorsed, certified, or otherwise approved by TMDB. The IMDb, Rotten Tomatoes and Letterboxd buttons open those sites; CinemAlert shows no scores of its own.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will appear here with a new effective date. If a change would mean collecting any personal information, the app will ask you first. For example, if CinemAlert ever adds a small banner ad, this policy will be updated before that version comes out, and the app will ask for any consent the ad needs. CinemAlert will never show pop-up or video ads.
